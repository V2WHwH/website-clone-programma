The work

From the stage to the florist, from a foundation to a mobile app: six chosen adventures, all custom-built.

Also: Nestlé · Gaznat · BG Ingénieurs Conseils · Centre technique horloger · ASPCo · Ligue Pulmonaire VD/VS

  1. 01 · Rieder Fleurs · Retail & craft

    Online shop

    “The store that extends the shop.”

    Launched during the pandemic, when shops had to close: an online store was needed, fast. PrestaShop as a proven foundation, shaped around the florist’s craft rather than the reverse.
  2. 02 · GottaFriend · Safety & mobile

    Personal safety application

    “Protect without collecting.”

    A virtual bodyguard. Up to three people close to you become your guardian angels and follow your route live; the alert reaches them in a single gesture. Emergency services stay one tap away, and your angels are notified at once.
  3. 03 · Massimo Furlan · Theatre & performance

    Company website, shows & tours

    “The work first, the interface in retreat.”

    Treat the site like a stage: the work first, the interface stepping back. A structure that lets the pieces breathe and keeps the memory of the work (tours, press) within reach.
  4. 04 · FOCAL · Training · Film & audiovisual

    Drupal site & custom tools

    “A site that works, not a showcase that sleeps.”

    A single Drupal foundation to carry the public site and connect it to the foundation’s in-house tools, instead of stacking disconnected solutions.
  5. 05 · UnitedHeroes · Sport & charity

    Charity auction platform

    “Legendary objects, causes that matter.”

    A bespoke auction engine: accounts, bids, payments. In service of the causes, not the other way round.
  6. 06 · Diabète Vaud, Promotion Santé VD & Ligue Pulmonaire · Public health

    Moving for your health: site & registrations

    “Three institutions, one voice.”

    Three health institutions, one tool. An events site where anyone finds their activity on a map and signs up in a few clicks, for the length of a canton-wide drive.

Who

A studio. A lab. One person to talk to.

First lines of code in 1996, in the early days of the web. More than twenty years in the craft, independent since 2014. Custom-built, because it is the only way to truly answer a need. I show the work, I explain the choices, I credit the people who work with me.

Born in Lausanne, I played hockey before living it from the stands, faithful to the LHC. In film, three masters: Wes Anderson, Jean-Pierre Jeunet, Quentin Tarantino. Worlds that belong to no one else. Music is vital to me, and concerts even more: that emotion that only exists together, when a whole room vibrates as one. The ice, the screen, the stage: three beats, like everything here. And underneath, the same curiosity, about everything, all the time. The one that opens every project with a question: what makes you distinct?

Nicolas Maeder
